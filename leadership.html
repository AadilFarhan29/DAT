Leadership

The team behind the trade.

Chairman
Mashood
Founder of Al Mazhar Group and Chairman of DAT International, Mashood has spent over 35 years building some of the UAE's most trusted distribution businesses. Under his leadership, DAT continues that legacy — growing into a diversified distributor of toys, lifestyle products, and everyday essentials, guided by the same commitment to quality and long-term partnerships.
"We built this on trust — with our partners, our customers, and each other. That doesn't change as we grow."
Vice Chairman
Mahroof
As Vice Chairman of DAT International, Mahroof has been instrumental in shaping the company's strategy and growth — expanding its market presence across the UAE and strengthening partnerships as a trusted supplier of toys, lifestyle products, home accessories, and everyday essentials. Working alongside the Al Mazhar Group leadership, he continues to drive DAT's focus on quality, innovation, and long-term value.
"Every new market, every new partnership — it all comes back to the same question: are we still delivering what we promised?"
